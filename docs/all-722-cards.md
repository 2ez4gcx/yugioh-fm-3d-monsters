# All 722 cards

In the original game **82 cards never drop** from any opponent, and **122 cards cost 999,999 star chips** (the star-chip cap; a won duel gives 1-5 chips), 24 of them without any password at all. Both patches (1 card per win and 5 cards per win) change this as follows.

- **Drops:** each of the 82 cards is added to the **S/A-POW and S/A-TEC** drop tables of up to 3 opponents who use that card in their own deck. The cards no opponent uses drop from the user of a related card (Gate Guardian Ritual with Gate Guardian, Javelin Beetle Pact with Javelin Beetle) or from the final bosses Heishin 2nd, DarkNite and Nitemare (Hungry Burger, Mask of Shine & Dark, Dark Magic Ritual, Magician of Black Chaos). Weight per draw: 8/2048 for level 7+, Exodia pieces, magic, trap, ritual and equip cards; 16/2048 for the others. One S/A win gives such a card with a chance of about 0.4% (8/2048) or 0.8% (16/2048) with the 1-card patch (one draw: about 256 or 128 wins per card on average), and 1.9% or 3.8% with the 5-card patch (five draws: about 52 or 26 wins). The other cards of the same table were scaled down in proportion to make room: a whole table lost at most 10.2% (Nitemare's table); because of rounding a few small weights lost a little more. Every card that could drop before still drops; the B/C/D tables and the opponents' decks are unchanged.
- **Note:** Goddess of Whim and Barrel Lily drop only from opponents whose story duels are optional (Teana 2nd, Villager 2, Villager 3); if you skip those duels you can still buy them (100 and 40 star chips).
- **Prices:** every card that cost 999,999 now has a price by level: level 1-2: 50, level 3: 100, level 4: 200, level 5: 400, level 6: 800, level 7: 1500, level 8: 2500, level 9-10: 4000, level 11-12: 6000; magic, trap, ritual and equip cards: 1000; each Exodia piece: 3000. The other prices are unchanged. Each password still works once.
- **New passwords:** the 24 cards without a password now use the real Yu-Gi-Oh! card passwords (from Yugipedia and YGOPRODeck; Psycho-Puppet and Mask of Shine & Dark exist only in video games, their passwords are Yugipedia's).
